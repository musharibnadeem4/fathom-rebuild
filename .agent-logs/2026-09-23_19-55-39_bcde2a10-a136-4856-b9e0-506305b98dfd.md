**Key design choices:**
- `utterances` has both `sequence` (stable render order) and `start_ms` (seek/scrub), each separately indexed with `meeting_id` — rendering a transcript and jumping to a video timestamp are different query shapes.
- `participants.talk_time_seconds` is denormalized (computed once at ingest from utterances) so talk-time views don't aggregate the transcript on every load.
- `summaries` has a unique `(meeting_id, template)` index, matching "one per meeting per template."
- `action_items.is_low_confidence` is the confirmed/suggestion flag; `owner_participant_id` (nullable, `set null` on delete) plus a freeform `owner_name` handles cases where the AI's owner guess doesn't cleanly match a diarized speaker.
- `coaching_flags` has a `metadata` jsonb column beyond kind/timestamp/label — needed to carry kind-specific context (e.g. which participants are involved in a talk-time imbalance) without one nullable column per kind.
- `highlights.public_slug` is unique/nullable — null until promoted to a public clip link, distinct from `shares`, which is per-recipient with its own `access_token`.
- All child tables cascade-delete from `meetings`; `action_items.owner_participant_id` uses `set null` instead, so deleting a participant doesn't destroy the action item itself.
